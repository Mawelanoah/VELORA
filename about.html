About Velora Fragrances

Our story and commitment to quality

Velora Fragrances was created to bring a refined, trustworthy online fragrance experience to South African shoppers. We believe finding a signature scent should feel considered, elegant and straightforward.

Our focus is on carefully selected fragrances — clearly distinguished between original designer pieces (once verified) and high-quality inspired alternatives. Transparency matters: we never misrepresent a product.

This website is currently running with demo product data. Real inventory, pricing, authenticity verification and delivery partners will be confirmed by the business before live trading.

Our Values

  • • Secure, transparent checkout when payments go live
  • • Responsive customer support via WhatsApp
  • • Clear policies on delivery and returns
